I am a Computer Science student at the University of Washington.

I'm interested in the full path that research data takes: the hardware that records it in the field, the networks and servers that move and store it, and the machine learning that turns it into answers. Outside the lab, I run my own self-hosted servers on Raspberry Pis, and before UW I built computer vision projects.

At Echospace, I work on the passive acoustics side of the lab, where I connect code and hardware for low-power acoustic recorders, test how they perform in the field, and help turn their recordings into usable data.
